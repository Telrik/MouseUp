Early, untested experimental build for macOS on Apple silicon and Intel. This is separate from the main Windows release.

Download `MouseUp-macOS-beta.zip`, extract it and move `MouseUp.app` to Applications. The build is ad-hoc signed and is not notarized by Apple; Gatekeeper may prevent it from opening.

The cursor is a click-through overlay. The normal macOS cursor and game-drawn cursors may remain visible. Fullscreen behavior, display scaling, shortcuts, and compatibility with other apps have not been verified on a Mac.

The included cursor artwork originates from YoloMouse; redistribution rights have not been established. MouseUp is independent and not affiliated with YoloMouse.
